Features

Made for the remote, loved on every screen

Khokhar TV turns your Xtream Codes or M3U service into a living-room experience that feels like premium streaming apps, without the lag.

Live TV that behaves like TV

Press Up and Down to change channel, type a number to jump, and press Left or Right for a see-through channel list that opens right where you are.

  • Channel up and down, number entry, last channel
  • Categories and favorites for fast zapping
  • Hold OK to switch picture size: fit, fill, stretch
  • Quietly reconnects if a stream stalls

Everything included

No add-ons and no ads. Every feature comes with every plan.

TV guide (EPG)

Programme grid with now, next and later, plus reminders that pop up before a show starts.

Multi-view

Two live channels at once in side-by-side, stacked or picture-in-picture layouts. Audio follows the active screen.

Continue watching

Films and episodes save your spot every few seconds and resume on any device you're signed in to.

Subtitles & audio

Change subtitle and audio language in place without restarting. Pick subtitle size and background.

Google voice search

Ask Google Assistant to play a channel or film on Khokhar TV and it opens straight into playback.

Parental PIN

Lock chosen categories behind a PIN that stays on your device.

Fast on cheap boxes

Hardware decoding renders straight to the screen, so even budget 4K boxes stay cool and smooth.

Synced everywhere

Favorites, history and progress follow your account across TV, phone and computer.

Remote-first design

Big focus states and simple navigation. Works with any basic remote, no keyboard needed.

Try it on your TV

Install in a couple of minutes and see how smooth it runs on your own box.
