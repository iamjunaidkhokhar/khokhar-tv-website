Plan questions

How do I pay?

On Android, subscribe inside the app and Google Play handles the payment securely. You can also message us on WhatsApp to arrange your account directly.

Can I use one account on several devices?

Yes. Sign in on your TV, phone and computer. Your plan sets how many screens can play at the same time.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions bought in the app are managed in the Play Store app under Subscriptions. Cancel any time before your renewal date.

What does the subscription include?

Full access to the Khokhar TV app and the service assigned to your account. Khokhar TV is a player and doesn't sell individual channels.
