Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Khokhar TV ("the app", "we", "us") is a media player app for Android TV / Google TV that plays IPTV streams (Xtream Codes and M3U playlists) provisioned to your account. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

Data we collect

  • Account info — email address, and display name/photo if you sign in with Google (via Supabase Authentication).
  • Subscription status — whether your account is active, and (if you subscribe through the app) a Google Play purchase token used to verify and renew your subscription. We never see or store your payment card details — Google Play handles the transaction.
  • Watch history & favorites — titles/channels you watch and your playback position, so "Continue Watching" and favorites work and sync across your devices.
  • Device/session data — a device identifier and last-seen timestamp, used only to enforce the number of simultaneous screens allowed on your plan.
  • Crash & diagnostic data — via Sentry: device model, OS version, and crash stack traces, to help us fix bugs. This does not include the content of your playlists.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show third-party ads or run ad-tracking SDKs.
  • We don't collect data from children knowingly; the app isn't directed at children under 13.

Third-party content

Khokhar TV does not host, produce or store any video content. Live channels, movies and series are streamed from IPTV server(s) assigned to your account by the operator; you're responsible for having the right to access that content. We are not responsible for the availability, legality or accuracy of third-party streams.

Data retention & deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data (watch history, favorites, subscription record), contact us at junaid.iam.khokhar@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +92 319 1187078. We'll action deletion requests within 30 days, except where we're required to retain records (e.g. billing) for a legal purpose.

Services we use

Supabase (authentication, database and server-side functions), Sentry (crash reporting), and Google Play Billing for subscriptions. Each is bound by its own privacy commitments as our data processor.

Contact
