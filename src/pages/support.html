Setup guides & FAQ

Stuck? Most answers are below. If not, message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you get watching.

{{icon:firetv}}

Install on Amazon Fire TV

  1. From the Fire TV home screen, search for Downloader and install it.
  2. Go to Settings ▸ My Fire TV ▸ Developer options ▸ Install unknown apps and turn on Downloader. (No Developer options? Open About and press OK on your device name seven times.)
  3. Open Downloader, type {{host}}/apk and press Go.
  4. When the download finishes, choose Install, then Open.
{{icon:tv}}

Install on Android TV & Google TV

  1. Install Downloader (or any browser) from the Play Store on your TV.
  2. Open Settings ▸ System ▸ About and press OK on Android TV OS build seven times to unlock developer mode.
  3. Go to Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Security & restrictions ▸ Unknown sources and allow Downloader.
  4. Open Downloader, enter {{host}}/apk, then install.
{{icon:phone}}

Install on Android phone or tablet

  1. Open the download page on your phone and tap Download APK.
  2. Open the file when it finishes. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then sign in.
{{icon:windows}}

Install on Windows or Linux

  1. Download the build for your system from the download page.
  2. Windows: unzip the folder and run iptv_pro.exe. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info ▸ Run anyway.
  3. Linux: extract the archive, install libmpv2 if needed, and run ./iptv_pro.

Frequently asked questions

Does Khokhar TV include channels?

Khokhar TV is a media player. It plays the IPTV service assigned to your account (Xtream Codes or M3U) and doesn't host or sell channels itself. You're responsible for having the right to access the content you play.

Which devices are supported?

Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, Android phones and tablets, Windows 10 and 11, and 64-bit Linux. Apple devices are in development.

How do I get updates?

The Android app checks for new versions and offers to update itself. On desktop, download the newest build from the download page.

A channel keeps buffering. What can I do?

Try a wired connection or move the box closer to your router, then reopen the channel. The app reconnects once automatically when a stream stalls. If one channel keeps failing while others play, let us know on WhatsApp.

How do I change subtitles or audio language?

While a film is playing, press Up or Down on your remote to open tracks. On series, hold Up or Down. On touch devices, tap the subtitles button.

How do I watch two channels at once?

Open Multi-view from the home screen. Use Left and Right to pick a screen, Up and Down to change its channel, and hold OK to change the layout.

How do I delete my account?

Message us on WhatsApp or by email and we'll remove your account and data. Details are in the privacy policy.
